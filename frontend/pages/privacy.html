Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Tattu Care ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Donation information: name (optional), Mobile Money number, donation amount, email (for receipts).
  • Contact form: name, email, and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Technical: IP address and timestamp, used for security and rate limiting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process donations through MTN Mobile Money.
  • To send donation receipts and acknowledgements.
  • To respond to questions submitted through the contact form.
  • To send periodic updates if you have subscribed to our newsletter.
  • For our own internal record-keeping and reporting.

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • MTN Mobile Money - to process your donation.
  • Government / regulatory authorities - where required by law.

4. Data Retention

Donation records are retained for at least 7 years to comply with financial reporting requirements. Contact messages are retained for up to 2 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal record-keeping obligations).
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time.

6. Security

We use HTTPS where possible, restrict admin access by password, and store sensitive donation data on secure servers. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Cookies

This site uses a session cookie only when an administrator logs in to manage content. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Contact

For privacy-related requests, email our team or call our office.
